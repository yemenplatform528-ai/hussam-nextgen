# Hussam NextGen — Complete Capability Map

The map is the single planning authority for the unified platform. It covers the
publicly documented capability families of large marketplace networks and the
additional controls required by Hussam's architecture.

## Commerce
Catalog, product identity, SKUs, variations, listings, seller offers, Featured
Offer, pricing rules, promotions, coupons/deals, bundles, subscriptions, B2B
pricing, quantity pricing, cart, unified multi-seller checkout, orders,
cancellations, returns, partial refunds and reviews.

## Seller operations
Onboarding, verification, Seller Center, products, inventory, warehouses,
orders, fulfillment, finance, settlements, payouts, customers, account health,
reports, notifications, promotions, brands, advertising and integrations.

## Network and fulfillment
Multi-location inventory, reservations, transfers, service areas, pickup
points, shipping quotes, shipments, tracking events, carrier adapters,
fulfillment methods, proof of delivery and delivery exceptions.

## Trust and service
Policy engine, seller health, risk signals, moderation, disputes, customer
cases, appeals, review integrity and audit trails.

## Growth and brand
Promotions, brands, brand stores/content, advertising campaigns/ad groups/
targets, attribution and controlled spend/billing foundations.

## Platform integration
Scoped integration applications, OAuth boundary, feeds/bulk jobs, reports,
notifications, webhooks and rate-limited external APIs.

## Intelligence
AI provider abstraction, AI runs, tool proposals, authorization/approval,
audit, evaluation hooks and HUS safe compilation/execution boundaries.

## Governance
Tenant isolation, authorization, lifecycle, idempotency, immutable financial
records, event/outbox delivery, security contracts, configuration and
operational observability.
